About

I am the founder of EM Conseil Invest, an independent advisory practice in investment and wealth strategy for individuals and entrepreneurs, and Sales Specialist France for Certu Systems EMEA. Both roles rest on the same foundation: nearly six years in the French Navy (Marine Nationale) as an armament and rescue-equipment technician, where precision, procedure and accountability were not optional. After the service, I applied that discipline to commercial work: real estate, connected concierge services, then founding Conciergerie Solea, a bespoke concierge company on the Côte d'Azur. Training in finance and an MBA in tourism and travel management (ESCAET) tie the threads together: understanding what clients need, structuring the offer rigorously, and following through on every commitment.
