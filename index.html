About

I live and work in Toulouse. I run two advisory practices, sell logistics software for the French market, and carry out quantitative research on my own account.

Boost Pilotage works with small and mid-sized companies on management control and treasury steering: reading the figures a business already produces, turning them into the indicators its manager decides on, and following the cash position month by month. EM Conseil Invest advises individuals and entrepreneurs on investment and wealth strategy. The two stay distinct. One works on a company, its management and its cash; the other on a person and their assets. At Certu Systems EMEA I do business development on the French market for a supply chain software vendor.

I served nearly seven years in the French Navy (Marine Nationale) as an armament and rescue-equipment technician, from airborne munitions for the Rafale Marine at Landivisiau to level-2 maintenance of individual and collective rescue equipment, ending as deputy manager of the parachute and rescue-equipment workshop aboard the aircraft carrier Charles de Gaulle. There I was a certifier for return to service, the person who signs that a piece of equipment is fit to fly.

Business development is the thread. I have sold in my own name before selling for others: property with IAD France, connected concierge services with OuiKey, and then a company of my own. I created Conciergerie Soléa on the French Riviera, ran it for a year and sold it in October 2025, handling quotes and invoicing, coordination of service providers, service quality, prospecting and the client relationship throughout. I then took that company as an object of study. The thesis that came out of it asks under what conditions an independent operator holds its ground against platform-driven networks and against owner self-management, and answers from market analysis of the Hyères, Giens and Toulon micro-markets, field observation, and the company's own operating data over its first year. It is the research work attached to my ESCAET MBA, a level 7 RNCP title obtained through validation of prior experience (VAE), and can be read in full under Writing.

Since December 2024 I have run an independent research programme on intraday systematic strategies for CME index futures and crypto venues, from tick-level and full-depth order-book data. Ten working papers are in preparation. Two of them are refutations of signals I had tested myself, and one describes two defects in my own research harness that had manufactured an apparent edge. They are listed under Research.

Research

Independent research on intraday systematic strategies for CME index futures and crypto venues, run on my own account from tick-level and full-depth order-book data. Every hypothesis is pre-registered before the data is touched, split in and out of sample, corrected for the number of variants tried, and compared against surrogate series. Ten working papers are in preparation; drafts are available on request at contact@boostpilotage.fr.

A Validation Doctrine for Retail-Scale Systematic Futures Research

Methodology · foundation paper · PDF on request

The protocol every other paper in this series is held to. Pre-registration of the hypothesis before the data is touched, a strict in-sample / out-of-sample split, correction for the number of variants tried, and surrogate testing to establish what a null result looks like on the same series.

Order Flow Does Not Predict: A Systematic Refutation of Eight Intraday Signals on NQ Futures

Signal research · refutation · PDF on request

Eight intraday order-flow signals (DeltaSwapRSI, absorption, CVD/price divergence, book imbalance, sweeps and an RSI 65/35 variant among them), tested across two seasons of MNQ and NQ data against surrogate series. None survives. Each either fails to clear transaction costs or is accounted for by drift tails in a rising regime. The paper records what was tested, how, and precisely where each signal breaks.

Two Backtest Bugs That Manufactured Edge

Research engineering · PDF on request

Two defects in my own research harness, each of which produced something that looked like an edge. An NT8 feed handler silently dropped roughly 42% of trades and returned fictitious profit factors; an ORB2 parameter grid returned identical P&L vectors across configurations that were supposed to be distinct. Both are described in full, together with the invariant assertion now permanently built into the harness to catch their recurrence.

Distance-Matched Placebo Testing: Refuting the Gap-Fill Edge

Methodology · refutation · PDF on request

The apparent gap-fill edge tested against a placebo built to match the distance distribution of real gaps, the counterfactual a naive test leaves out. Once the comparison is matched, the effect does not survive.

Spread Dynamics on MNQ: Why Pure Spread Capture Is Not Viable at Retail Scale

Microstructure · measurement · PDF on request

The MNQ spread sits at one tick roughly 42% of the time, with a median of two, against round-turn costs of two ticks. Pure spread capture is arithmetically closed at retail scale, and the paper sets out the move to a directional grid that followed from the measurement.

How Many Ticks Is a Book-Imbalance Signal Actually Worth on ES and NQ?

Microstructure · execution cost · PDF on request

A book-imbalance signal, I_N, measured on full-depth MBO data for ES and NQ. The signal predicts, taker execution consumes what it predicts, and the paper quantifies that distance in ticks.

GLFT Market Making: Anchoring the Infinite-Horizon Solution and Why (T-t) Is a Trap

Market making · calibration · PDF on request

From Avellaneda-Stoikov to the Guéant-Lehalle-Fernandez-Tapia infinite-horizon solution, with A and κ calibrated on live flow (γ = 1, A = 760.8, κ = 189.67). One anomaly is left unresolved: the reservation price sits below the mid while inventory is under-weighted.

Latency Economics for a Retail-Scale Operation

Infrastructure · PDF on request

Review · life cycle of an edge · PDF on request

Thesis

Designing and running sustainable tourism operations, in France and abroad

Thesis submitted for the VAE, academic year 2025 · ESCAET, MBA Manager des Activités du Tourisme et des Voyages, level 7 RNCP title

The thesis asks how an independent short-let concierge business can be built to be profitable, sustainable and replicable. It draws on the first year of Conciergerie Soléa on the French Riviera, combining market analysis, field observation and internal operating data across the Hyères, Giens and Toulon micro-markets. Its argument is that an independent operator holds its ground against both platform-driven networks and owner self-management only when three things hold together: operations standardised into documented, repeatable procedures; decisions driven by measured data rather than intuition; and a commercial pitch built on evidence, projected net revenue per night and service-level commitments, rather than on promises. The closing section examines the transfer of skills from military aeronautical supply chain to tourism management, and sets out the conditions under which such a model can be duplicated to other territories without diluting it.
